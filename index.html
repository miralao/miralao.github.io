MAIN CHARACTERS:

HANNELORE PFÄHLERIN (55) is a surfer, slacker and clinical hoarder of everything under the Sun. German-Australian and raised in the Outback, the Pfählerins immigrated to the US by road trip in ‘79. Today, Hanelore runs a failing record shop in Isla Vista, California, and keeps it afloat with random junk that seems to stick to her--some from the beach, some from a mysterious “elsewhere”.

This “unheard music” aficionado and part-time petty thief is rarely seen without her ratty mountaineering backpack, her freaky giant sunhat, and a cracked Walkman. It’s not her just being vintage to look vintage, she scored it out of the trash behind Zócalo.

Hannelore is blonde with pale blue eyes, has tanned skin with sunspots, and wears a gapped, wrinkled smile. Her molars have been replaced by glinting gold teeth.

IFECHIMAMANDA DIKE-ABALỊ (49) is a freaked-out, cute little bundle of nerves. The highlight of her day is always going to see Hannelore on the beach.

After graduating from Stanford with an MBA in 2000, Ifechi moved to Santa Barbara to get a bachelor’s in biology, then completed another bachelor’s in environmental science in 2008. She blanked on what to actually do with her extra education, and wound up at a local actuary firm in 2011.

Ifechimamanda has green eyes and black curly hair, and wears round golden glasses.
