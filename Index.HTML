KILL ✩ THE ✩ SUN Welcome to Goma! Our peoples live on Goma, or Earth(6,5)--a “blended family” multiverse planet of anachronistic time periods, remembered places; of thoughts, feelings, and art. Earth(6,5) is a subverse of the Spider-Verse, which itself is located on the Central Laniakean Curve of 2-dimensional vector space. Angels, demons, gods, witches, humans, and monsters are the six paraphyletic clades of amniotes, or sentient life. They are descended from the blood, sweat, and tears of Hiro I. Since 1993, Goma has been ruled by the Cerulean State, comprised solely of the Chancellor, the Cabinet, and the Control Yuan. What follows is their constitution (6,5)§1: THE ONE STATE ACT PREAMBLE WHEREAS, anarchy, monarchy, democracy are all unacceptable to the Public and to the Public Good, THEREFORE, they shall be combined into One State, NOW, you just have to philosophically and legally define it! Come aboard! THE PUBLIC The Public are all those amniotes that make up sentient life. THE PUBLIC GOOD The Public Good is the wellbeing of the whole and parts of the Central Laniakean Curve.
