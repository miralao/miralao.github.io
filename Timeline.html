185 BC- The latter point of divergence. The Maurya Empire never collapses, and instead has a resurgence.
404 AD- Maurya, having conquered most of Asia, takes advantage of the fall of Rome to conquer Southern Europe as well.
1000- Goryeo Dynasty and Maurya run afoul of each other and clash.
1004- The Great War begins between the two powers.
1066: Norman Conquest, beginning the 900 Years' War
1106- Pampanga Accords are signed, ending the Great War.
1215- To celebrate 99 years of peace, the two powers unify into the Gorya Empire.
1744- Great Schism collspdes the Gorya Empire into three kingdoms: Evropa, Mongolia, and Maruya. Its colonies become independent or are picked up by other empires.
1789: "Déclaration des droits de l'Homme en Haiti et dans le monde" penned by A. Lafeyette Haititon
1791-1804: Haitian Rebellion
1815-1835: Napoleonic Wars
1914- Archduke Franz Ferdinand is killed, sparking the Long War in the European theater.
1924- Long War ends, Berlin Accords are signed between the Central Powers and Outer Powers.
1945- France and Germany join a Perpetual Union with Eirce, ending nine centuries of Erbfeindschaft and laying the groundwork for the EU.
1966: EU formed.
