KILL ✩ THE ✩ SUN

Welcome to Goma!

Our peoples live on Goma, or Earth(6,5)--a “blended family” multiverse planet of anachronistic time periods, remembered places; of thoughts, feelings, and art. Earth(6,5) is a subverse of the Spider-Verse, which itself is located on the Central Laniakean Curve of 2-dimensional vector space.

Angels, demons, gods, witches, humans, and monsters are the six paraphyletic clades of amniotes, or sentient life. They are descended from the blood, sweat, and tears of Hiro I.

Since 1993, Goma has been ruled by the Cerulean State, comprised solely of the Chancellor, the Cabinet, and the Control Yuan. What follows is their constitution (6,5)§1:

THE ONE STATE ACT
PREAMBLE

WHEREAS, anarchy, monarchy, democracy are all unacceptable to the Public and to the Public Good,

THEREFORE, they shall be combined into One State,

NOW, you just have to philosophically and legally define it!

Come aboard!

THE PUBLIC

The Public are all those amniotes that make up sentient life.

THE PUBLIC GOOD

The Public Good is the wellbeing of the whole and parts of the Central Laniakean Curve.

STYLE & COLOR KEYS

Concepts

Kill the Sun will have a fluid art style, similar to works such as Spiderverse. Each Zone will have a different art style to accompany its atmosphere.

Characters will have consistent designs that remain constant across style and location, with only their lighting and texture changing as they cross World Walls.

Act 1

Popo’s House

Stark, dark and saturated colors. Ominous, symbolic looking shapes resembling stained glass or murals. Soviet animation and “Hollow Mind” influences this.

The Beach

ex. the beach in Steven Universe. This will have a semi complex cartoony style, ex. The Owl House and She-Ra (2018). Also has lighting and color keying like Klaus.
